# Raising domain rating: backlink plan (2026-10-07)

## Why DR is 4

Ahrefs Domain Rating is computed **only** from links on other sites: how many distinct
domains link to dopplervpn.org and how strong those domains are. Nothing on our own pages
changes it. Pages showing "20" or "40" in Ahrefs are URL Rating (the strength of one page
that earned a link) or the DR of a site that links to us, not our DR.

On-site work (internal links, the protocol hub, technical fixes) decides how the authority
we do earn flows to the pages that should rank. Only new referring domains move DR itself.

## Linkable assets we now have

- `/vpn-protocols` and its ten guides (EN, RU): a neutral, cited protocol comparison that
  links out to the apps it covers. Resource pages like this are what people cite.
- `/tools/what-is-my-ip`, `/tools/dns-leak-test`, `/tools/webrtc-leak-test`: free tools
  people link to from guides and forums.
- `/how-it-works/vless-reality-tunnel`: the long technical article.

## Content hygiene that affects ranking (not DR)

- Three near-duplicate posts about the "OpenVPN sunset" exist
  (`the-openvpn-sunset-...-mlyakeg8`, `the-openvpn-sunset-...-mly0xy4i`,
  `the-openvpn-exodus-...-mlwv4gkh`). Keep the strongest, 301 the others to it.
- Skip off-topic head terms from keyword tools: "tubi" and "pluto tv" are streaming brands;
  "vpn" and "vpn service" are out of reach at this authority.
