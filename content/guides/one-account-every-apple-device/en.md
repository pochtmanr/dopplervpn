> **The short version.** Your Doppler account is a random ID, not an email address. Enter the same ID on each iPhone, iPad and Mac and they share one plan, up to 10 devices. The Apple Watch app follows your iPhone and does not count as a separate device.

## Your account is an ID, not an email

The first time you open Doppler, the app makes up an account ID in the form `VPN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`. There is no name, email, phone number or password behind it. That is the privacy point of the design: there is nothing personal on file to leak, sell or hand over.

The flip side is that the ID is the key. Anyone who has it can sign in as you, and if you lose it and have no other way back in, we cannot look it up by your name, because we never had your name. Keep it somewhere safe, such as your password manager.

You find it under **Profile** in the app. Many of us learned this habit the hard way in the era of ICQ numbers, when a forgotten UIN meant starting over with an empty contact list. Write it down once and you will not have that problem.

## Add your other Apple devices

Doppler is one App Store app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, so a plan bought on any of them covers all of them.

1. Install Doppler on the new device.
2. When it opens, choose to log in with an existing account and enter your account ID.
3. Connect. The new device now shares your plan.

If you bought your plan through the App Store, **Restore Purchases** in the app also brings it back on a device signed in to the same Apple ID.

## The Devices screen

**Devices** lists everything signed in to your account: the device name, its platform, and when it was last active. A bar at the top shows how many of your 10 slots are in use, with a count per platform. The device you are holding is marked **This Device**, and the first device on the account is marked **Main**.

To free a slot, remove a device you no longer use with the button on its row. Removing a device signs it out; it does not cancel your plan or touch any other device.

## Where Apple Watch fits

The [Apple Watch app](/guides/vpn-on-apple-watch) is a remote for the VPN on your iPhone. It has no tunnel and no account of its own, so it does not take a slot. It can show your device list and your plan, but it never shows your full account ID, and removing devices stays on the phone.

## Android and Windows too

The same account ID works in Doppler for Android and Windows. A plan bought through the App Store is managed in your Apple ID settings, wherever else you use it.
