# Draft — privacy retention for support contacts

Version: 2026-09-28. Status: draft. Not a new published retention period.

The public privacy page now says that a support or recovery contact is not the same as deleting the account, and that payment records may be kept where the law requires it. It does not publish a new retention schedule.

Still undecided:

- How long a support contact is kept after a removal request.
- The public URL for removing only a support contact. `contactRemovalPublicUrl` is still null.
- Which payment records must be kept, and for how long, beyond the existing published privacy text.
