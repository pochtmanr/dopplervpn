# Draft — OxaPay fee formula is unresolved

Version: 2026-09-28. Status: draft. Not an approved customer promise.

OxaPay invoice and payment-information APIs can show what was paid. They do not create a right to keep a network fee, an exchange-rate change, or a gateway fee.

Missing before any formula is published:

- The merchant agreement and refund or payout capability.
- Asset, network, decimal precision, and payout minimum.
- Whether a return fee exists and who pays it.
- The wallet address the customer names. Do not refund to a guessed sender address.

The working example stays $6.99 gross. $6.84 is not the formula.
