# Draft — not an approved customer promise

Version: 2026-09-28. Status: draft. Do not publish this file and do not load it into the support bot.

## Proposal

When a person approves a refund, return the gross amount the customer paid. For a charge of $6.99 with a processor cost of $0.15, the customer amount is $6.99. The $0.15 stays a merchant expense. Do not use $6.84.

This is a proposal for a future goodwill rule. It does not replace the published August 2026 statement until the owner accepts it.
